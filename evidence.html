Our Commitment to Evidence

At Koloso, we believe that effective education technology must be grounded in rigorous evidence. We are committed to understanding what works, what doesn't, and why.

Our approach combines ongoing data collection from partner schools, independent research partnerships, and transparent reporting of our findings. We don't just measure outputs — we measure real learning outcomes and teacher experiences.

This commitment drives continuous improvement in our platform and ensures that every feature we build is designed to create genuine impact in classrooms across Zambia and beyond.
