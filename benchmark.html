Assess Learning with Confidence

Koloso Benchmark is a powerful assessment tool that measures where a child is in their learning journey. Whether you're a school enrolling new students, a parent wanting clarity on your child's progress, or a government tracking national education outcomes, Koloso Benchmark provides the insights you need.

Who Uses Koloso Benchmark?

Schools

Accurately assess incoming students to place them in the right class and identify learning gaps from day one. No more guesswork when a new learner joins your school.

Parents

Get a clear, objective picture of where your child stands academically. Understand their strengths, identify areas for improvement, and track their progress over time.

Governments

Assess learning outcomes at scale to understand the state of education nationwide. Track progress against SDGs and benchmark performance against other African countries.

Choose Your Assessment Standard

Benchmark learners against local or international curricula

Local

Local Curriculum Assessment

Assess learners against the Zambian national curriculum standards. Perfect for understanding how students perform relative to local educational expectations and Ministry of Education requirements.

  • Ministry of Education standards
  • Local grade-level benchmarks
  • National comparison data
Global

Global Curriculum Assessment

Measure learners against international standards like Cambridge curriculum. Ideal for international schools, parents considering education abroad, or tracking global competitiveness.

  • Cambridge curriculum aligned
  • International benchmarks
  • Global comparison data
  • Multi-country performance metrics
