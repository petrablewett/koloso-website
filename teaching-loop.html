The Teaching Loop

The Koloso Teaching Support System - Helping teachers at every step

1

Plan & Teach

Stop spending evenings writing lesson plans. Koloso generates a complete, curriculum-aligned scheme of work for your class and turns it into ready-to-use weekly lesson plans — structured, dated, and built around your national syllabus. You review, personalise, and teach. The planning is done.

2

Assess & Analyse

Koloso gives you the right assessment for every moment — quick in-lesson checks, end-of-topic tests, homework, rapid-fire quizzes, and full end-of-term exams. It marks everything automatically and shows you exactly how your class is performing: which topics landed, which didn't, and which students need your attention most. Paper or digital, fast or thorough — the right tool is always there.

3

Adapt & Improve

Assessment data is only useful if it tells you what to do next. When your class is struggling with a concept, Koloso surfaces targeted teaching suggestions — specific, practical, and ready to use in the next lesson. No guesswork. No generic advice. Just a clear next step based on what your students actually need.

4

Guidance

Great teachers never stop learning. Koloso's built-in professional guidance library puts expert teaching notes on over thirty topics right inside the app — written in plain language, readable in under five minutes, available even without internet. Whether you're a first-year teacher building your craft or an experienced educator looking for a fresh approach, the support you need is always within reach.

5

Report

Progress reports for every student, generated automatically from the assessments already in your system. Parents receive clear, honest information about how their child is doing and how they can help at home. School leaders get class and grade-level summaries that make it easy to see what's working and where action is needed. The only thing you write is your personal comment — the part that makes a report worth reading.

6

Comply

When the inspector arrives, Koloso schools are ready. Signed schemes of work, dated lesson plans, complete assessment records, and issued progress reports — all tracked, all exportable, all in order. Your inspection readiness dashboard shows your status at a glance and generates a complete documentation pack at the tap of a button. Less stress, no scramble - just confidence.
